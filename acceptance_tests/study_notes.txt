PROJECT ULTRON ARCHITECTURE & OPERATING SYSTEMS STUDY NOTES

1. CPU Scheduling:
Operating Systems utilize CPU scheduling algorithms such as First-Come First-Served (FCFS), Shortest Job First (SJF), and Round Robin (RR) with time quantum allocations.

2. Memory Management:
Virtual Memory uses Paging and Segmentation. Pages are mapped via Page Tables to physical frames.

3. Process Synchronization:
Mutex locks and Semaphores prevent race conditions in concurrent multi-threaded applications.
